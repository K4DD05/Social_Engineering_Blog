01 / THE CONCEPT

What is Social Engineering?

Social engineering is a form of manipulation in which attackers exploit human psychology rather than relying solely on technical vulnerabilities. They use trust, fear, urgency, curiosity and authority to persuade people to perform actions that compromise security.

02 / ATTACK VECTORS

Know the techniques.

Social engineers use different approaches depending on their target and objective.

01DIGITAL

Phishing

Fraudulent emails, messages or websites designed to trick people into revealing sensitive information.

02MANIPULATION

Pretexting

Creating a believable false identity or scenario to convince someone to disclose information.

03DECEPTION

Baiting

Offering something attractive, such as free software or a USB drive, to encourage unsafe actions.

04PHYSICAL

Tailgating

Following an authorized person into a restricted physical location without proper permission.

05IDENTITY

Impersonation

Pretending to be a trusted individual, colleague or authority figure to gain someone's trust.

06VOICE

Vishing

Using phone calls or voice messages to manipulate victims into revealing confidential information.

03 / DEFENSIVE SECURITY

Don't become the entry point.

Verify identities

Confirm requests through trusted channels, especially when money or sensitive information is involved.

Question urgency

Attackers often create panic to prevent you from thinking carefully before acting.

Protect credentials

Never share passwords, authentication codes or confidential access information.

Report suspicious activity

Notify the appropriate security personnel when something feels unusual.
